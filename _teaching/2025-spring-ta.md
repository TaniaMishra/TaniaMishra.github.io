Course Description:
------
Fundamental concepts of operating systems including virtualization, process control and scheduling, concurrency and synchronization, memory management, file systems and device control. 3 hrs. lecture, 2 hrs. lab.

Responsibilities:
------
* Led a weekly 2-hour lab section of 11 students
* Guided students through projects, practice problems, and course material in weekly office hours 
* Proctored and graded lab practical exams and written midterm exams
* Worked with a team of 2 other undergraduate TAs, 1 graduate TA, and 1 professor
